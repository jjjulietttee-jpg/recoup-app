How to make a home inventory for insurance on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Recoup developer

Short answer. Create a room in Recoup, add each item with a photo, price, purchase date and serial number, then export the PDF report grouped by room. Free covers 5 items and 2 rooms; Pro removes the limits.

Step by step

  1. Create roomsAdd a room for each space: living room, kitchen, garage.
  2. Add itemsPhotograph each item and add its category, price, purchase date, serial number and receipt (Pro).
  3. Review totalsTotals per room and for the whole home update as you add items.
  4. Export the PDFGenerate a PDF with items grouped by room, photos, dates, serial numbers, receipts and totals, and share it.

Frequently asked questions

How many items can I add for free?

Five items and two rooms, with the full arithmetic. Pro unlocks unlimited items, receipts, serial numbers and the PDF.

Which room should I photograph next?

Recoup points at rooms whose inventory looks implausibly small so you know where to look next.
