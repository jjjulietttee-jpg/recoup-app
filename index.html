What Recoup does

  • The gap, in one numberType your policy limit once. Recoup totals what your documented belongings would cost to replace and shows the distance to that limit.
  • Category sub-limitsItems in categories policies usually cap (jewelry, watches, firearms, silver, bicycles) are marked: 'usually capped at around this amount, check your own policy'.
  • Depreciation (ACV) with workingEvery figure shows its arithmetic, for example $1,200 x 40% = $480, so you can redo it by hand.
  • PDF reportItems by room with photo, purchase date, serial number, receipt and totals, ready to export and share.
  • What to photograph nextRooms whose inventory looks implausibly small are highlighted.

Frequently asked questions

What does Recoup do?

It is a home inventory app that compares what your belongings would cost to replace with the personal-property limit you type from your policy, and shows the gap.

Is Recoup an insurer, appraiser or adviser?

No. It only does arithmetic on numbers you enter. Typical category caps are 'usually capped at around this amount': check your own policy.

Is there a free version?

Yes: 5 items and 2 rooms with the full arithmetic.

Does Recoup collect my data?

The App Store privacy label says no data is collected.
