How to work out the depreciated value (ACV) of your belongings on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Recoup developer

Short answer. Enter an item's price and purchase date in Recoup. It applies a typical service life for the category with a residual-value floor and shows the working, for example $1,200 x 40% = $480. Use it as an estimate and check your own policy for how it values losses.

Step by step

  1. Add the itemEnter category, price paid and purchase date.
  2. Read the valueRecoup shows replacement price, age factor and the resulting ACV on one line.
  3. See the floorEach category has a typical service life and a minimum residual value, so old items do not fall to zero.
  4. Compare with your limitThe Home screen sums everything and sets it against your policy limit.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an appraisal?

No. Recoup is not an appraiser. The figures are arithmetic on what you typed, using typical service lives.

Why show the working?

So you can redo any number by hand and see where it comes from.
