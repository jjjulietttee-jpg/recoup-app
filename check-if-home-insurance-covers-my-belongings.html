How to check if your home insurance covers your belongings on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Recoup developer

Short answer. Add your belongings to Recoup, type the limit from your policy once, and the app shows the gap between what it would cost to replace your documented things and that limit. Recoup only does arithmetic on numbers you enter. It is not an insurer, an appraiser or an adviser: check your own policy for its real limits.

Step by step

  1. Add what you ownOpen Recoup, pick a room and add items with a name, category, price paid and purchase date.
  2. Enter your policy limitOn the Coverage tab type the personal-property limit printed on your policy, as one number.
  3. Read the gapThe Home screen shows the total, the limit and the distance between them. Tap a number to see its working, for example $1,200 x 40% = $480.
  4. Check category capsItems in categories that policies usually cap (jewelry, watches, firearms, silver, bicycles) are marked with a note such as 'usually capped at around this amount: check your own policy'.

Frequently asked questions

Does Recoup tell me what my policy pays?

No. It does arithmetic on the numbers you enter and shows typical category caps. Your policy document is the only source for your real limits.

What is ACV?

Actual cash value: replacement cost minus depreciation for age. Recoup shows each depreciation step so you can redo it by hand.
