How to see which belongings pass typical policy sub-limits on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Recoup developer

Short answer. Recoup marks every item in a commonly capped category that passes a typical cap, worded as 'usually capped at around this amount: check your own policy'. The real sum is in your policy.

Step by step

  1. Add valuablesAdd jewelry, watches, silver or bicycles with their price.
  2. Open CoverageThe Coverage tab lists capped categories and the typical amount each is usually limited to.
  3. Spot the overshootAn item above a typical cap is flagged: a $6,000 ring inside a $250,000 policy may still be limited far below $6,000.
  4. Check your policyCompare against the sub-limits in your own policy or ask your insurer.
